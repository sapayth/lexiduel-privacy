# LexiDuel Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information the LexiDuel app ("the app") handles and why.
LexiDuel is published by **Sapayth Hossain** ("we", "us").

## Summary

- You play as a guest. There is no sign-up, no account and no login.
- Your game progress is stored **only on your device**. We do not run servers that receive it.
- The app shows **optional rewarded ads** through Google AdMob. Google may collect device
  information to serve and measure those ads, subject to the consent choices you make.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device so the game works:

- A random guest identifier and display name ("Guest") created on first launch
- Game progress: statistics, XP and level, streaks, campaign stars, achievements, daily goals
  and hint power-up inventory
- Settings such as Hard Mode

This data never leaves your device through the app itself. If Android backup is turned on for
your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup, which Google handles
under its own privacy policy. You can erase it at any time with **Settings → Reset Progress**,
or by uninstalling the app.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a hint power-up. Ads are
provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's
Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

- Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
- Approximate location derived from your IP address
- Device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language)
- Ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or completed)
- Diagnostic and crash information related to the ads SDK

How Google uses this information is described at
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites> and in Google's privacy policy at
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

### Your choices

- **Consent:** where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United
  Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.
  You can change that choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy Choices**.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings
  (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).
- **Not watching ads:** ads only appear when you tap to watch one. The game is fully playable
  without them.

## Children

LexiDuel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the
app, contact us and we will help remove it.

## Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information and we do not receive your game data. The only third
party that receives information from the app is Google, for the advertising described above.

## Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.
Information sent to Google by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example when online multiplayer is added), we will update this page
and its effective date, and describe significant changes in the app's release notes.
